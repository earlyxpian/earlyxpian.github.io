# A Language Update of Thelwall's translation of Tertullian's *On the Veiling of Virgins*

This is a major language update of a translation by Sydney Thelwall of Tertullian's *On the Veiling of Virgins*. This translation was originally published in 1870 and the language update was released in 2025. Both the original translation and the update are in the public domain. 

This book can be downloaded in the following formats:
