# Tertullian --- *On the Veiling of Virgins*

## Brief description:

Tertullian wrote the small work *On the Veiling of Virgins* to address a problem he saw in his local assembly where unmarried women would remove their head coverings when coming to the meetings of the assembly. He gives his arguments why he feels this practice is neither correct, not the historic Christian practice.
